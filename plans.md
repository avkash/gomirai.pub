# Plans

## GOmirai is free right now

There is no charge to use GOmirai today. Sign in, connect your mail, download the
model and use it — nothing is behind a payment.

New accounts start on **Basic Pro**, described below. Paid tiers exist in the
product and are listed here so you can see where it is heading, but they are not
being sold yet, and you will not be moved onto one without being asked.

Your plan is attached to your 650 AI Lab account and applies wherever you sign
in. It is shown in **About → Plan**.

## Notes on the limits

**Mail history** is how far back GOmirai backfills your archive. Independent of
the AIBox window.

**AIBox analysis window** is how far back the AI looks. It analyses a rolling
recent window plus everything new, rather than your whole archive — so a large
mailbox does not mean an enormous first run.

**Vault** is document memory — adding files so they are catalogued, searchable
and summarised. Basic Pro does not include it. The Obsidian notes vault is
separate and available on every plan.

**Mobile companion** is the read-only phone app that syncs your AIBox over an
end-to-end encrypted relay. Bundled with Ultimate.

**Fair use** on Ultimate means there is no hard cap, but the account is expected
to belong to one person.
